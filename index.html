DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM INTELLIGENCE & FORENSICS

Understand your ecosystem.
Anticipate the risks.
Know how to respond.

Digital environments are interconnected and constantly changing. I help organisations understand the terrain, investigate what is emerging, assess the exposure and build a response that holds under pressure.

THE QUESTIONS I HELP ANSWER

You cannot manage a risk you do not understand. Individual accounts, posts or incidents rarely tell the whole story. The relevant signals are often spread across teams, communities and platforms.

I help map the environment: the platforms, communities, actors, behaviours, narratives and connections that shape it. We separate an isolated event from something coordinated, and build a picture of where risk can emerge outside the organisation and still reach operations, users or reputation.

Not every emerging issue is a serious threat, and not every serious threat is visible at first.

I help identify the threat scenarios that matter, examine how the ecosystem could be exploited, and assess the gaps in processes, controls and operational capability. That shows where attention belongs now, which risks need a decision, and where prevention changes the exposure.

A high-risk event moves faster than the standing process. Online behaviour, public attention, emerging narratives and coordinated activity can change before the organisation has a shared picture.

I help set monitoring priorities, risk indicators, escalation thresholds, responsibilities and the decision structure. The aim is to see the development early, know what is actually happening, and act as one while the situation changes. Legal, public policy and communications stay in their roles. This work is the operational picture and the readiness around it.

This draws on digital risk investigations, high-risk event management and operational governance at a major global platform, including Trust & Safety and digital forensics.

LEADERSHIP COACHING & SPARRING

Better leadership. Stronger teams. Clearer decisions.

I work with managers, heads of function and senior specialists on stronger leadership and clearer decisions. The work is coaching, mentoring and independent sparring.

THE QUESTIONS I HELP ANSWER

As a leader, your impact increasingly depends on how well your organisation operates rather than how much you personally deliver.

We examine how responsibilities are distributed, how decisions are made, how performance is managed and where your attention creates the greatest value. The objective is to build a department that performs consistently without depending on your involvement in every decision.

Many of the decisions that matter sit outside your formal authority. You have to align people with different priorities, negotiate resources, meet resistance, and win support for a change you cannot simply instruct.

We work on the stakeholders, the conversation, and the negotiation that turns a position into a decision and a decision into execution.

Senior leadership often involves decisions without obvious answers. Competing stakeholder interests, organisational constraints and incomplete information make it difficult to evaluate options objectively.

I act as a sparring partner to help you structure the problem, challenge assumptions, consider consequences and develop a clear course of action. You retain ownership of the decision while benefiting from an experienced external perspective.

This draws on international leadership across distributed teams and demanding operational environments.

BUSINESS TRANSFORMATION & STRATEGY

From strategic ambition to operational performance.

Organisations rarely struggle because they lack objectives. The harder part is how strategy becomes structure, ownership and everyday execution.

THE QUESTIONS I HELP ANSWER

As priorities evolve, existing departmental structures, responsibilities and decision-making processes may no longer support what the organisation needs to achieve.

I help assess organisational design, functional responsibilities, reporting and decision structures, and cross-functional dependencies. The aim is to establish an operating model that connects organisational capabilities with strategic objectives.

A clear strategy can still fail when ownership is fragmented, priorities compete and execution lacks a consistent management framework.

I help translate strategic direction into operational priorities, accountable ownership, performance measures and execution routines. This makes it easier for leadership to see progress, identify obstacles and make informed trade-offs.

Transformation requires more than a target operating model or implementation roadmap. New structures and processes must work within the realities of the organisation and gain the support of the people responsible for delivering them.

I help connect organisational design with implementation, stakeholder alignment, capability development, adoption measurement and continuous improvement.

This combines international operational leadership, organisational governance, Lean Six Sigma and practical transformation experience.
